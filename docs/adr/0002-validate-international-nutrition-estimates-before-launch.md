# Validate international nutrition estimates before broad launch

The app aims to handle meals from many cuisines, with nutrient totals taking priority over exact food names. Before a broad public launch, a diverse pilot will compare pre-review and final reviewed nutrient totals separately against meals with weighed portions and known recipes. Numeric pass criteria will be chosen after a calibration pilot and applied to a larger validation set, because visual food identification and portion estimates vary across dishes and cuisines.
