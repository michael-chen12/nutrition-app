# Meal Nutrition Tracking

Terms used to describe personal meal tracking for general wellness.

## Language

**Nutrition estimate**:
An approximate amount of calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat attributed to pictured food after the person reviews it. It may carry an uncertainty flag.
_Avoid_: Calorie count, nutrition fact

**Meal entry**:
An editable, saved record of one photographed meal with its photos, reviewed foods and portions, and nutrition estimate.
_Avoid_: Scan

**Meal photo**:
One of the photos of food retained with a meal entry.

**Pictured food**:
Food visible across a meal entry's photos, counted once when the same food appears from multiple angles. It includes food that was shared or left uneaten.

**Meal history**:
The person's chronological collection of meal entries.
_Avoid_: Scan history

**Daily pictured-food total**:
The combined nutrition estimates for pictured food in meal entries assigned to one day. It is not a measure of food consumed by one person.
_Avoid_: Daily intake, calories eaten

**Custom food**:
A food added by the person with nutrition values they provide when no suitable reference food is available.
